Peace families order hot lunch once a month, manage before/after care, and pay

online — office staff get counts, attendance, and invoices without paperwork.

  • Parents: one page per month, Entrée / Alt / No lunch per child per day,

$4.50 every meal. Care drop-ins in two clicks.

  • Office: set the order window (e.g. 15th–25th, changeable monthly), close

grades for field trips, verify parents once, send invoices in one click.

  • Kitchen: daily counts by entrée, grade, and allergy flags. Printable.

Ordering runs in a monthly window set by the office; when it closes, late

changes go through the office only. Children are matched to parents through

guardian records and a one-time office verification, and payments run through

Vanco — no card data is ever stored.
