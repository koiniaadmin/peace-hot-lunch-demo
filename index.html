How signing in works
1. Click Sign in with Google and pick the Google account the school has on file as your guardian email — no new password to remember.
2. First visit: the office verifies you once (a quick ID check), matching your login to your children.
3. From then on you see only your own family — your kids, your balance, your invoices. Staff sign in with their school accounts instead.
Ordering runs in a monthly window set by the office; when it closes, late changes go through the office only. Children are matched to parents through guardian records and a one-time office verification, and payments run through Vanco — no card data is ever stored.
